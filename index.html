Md Abdullah Al Forhad

Ph.D. Candidate

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of North Texas. I conduct research in the Active Machine Learning (AML) Lab and the Learning, Language, Vision (LLaVi) Lab under the supervision of Dr. Weishi Shi. I completed my Master’s degree in Computer Science from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of North Texas with a 4.00 GPA.

My research focuses on deep active learning, continual lifelong learning, and multimodal learning, with the goal of making AI systems more adaptive, robust, and useful in real-world settings.
